About Me

Hi, I'm Marcio Belucci, a software development student at BYU-Pathway Worldwide from Brazil. I'm working toward my bachelor's degree, and I'm also earning a Web and Computer Programming certificate along the way.

I have a technical background in IT from Senac São Paulo and hands-on experience building real projects. I developed a full website for a lighting and decoration business using Python, Flask, SQL, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and I deployed it to the cloud. I also work as a Student Mentor at Springboard, helping other students succeed in their first semesters.

I speak Portuguese, Spanish and English. In this course, I want to strengthen my front-end skills and build a responsive website for the Piracicaba Chamber of Commerce.

My hobbies are fishing, playing basketball and watching movies.
